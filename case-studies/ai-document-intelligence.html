Document Intelligence Desk

A concept for reading incoming documents, extracting key fields and sending uncertain items to a person for review.

Concept project — demonstration only

CategoryAI Applications
StatusConcept, not a client project
Related serviceAI App Development
This concept explores how an AI tool could turn long, repetitive documents into structured information while keeping a person responsible for anything uncertain.

The hypothetical problem

Teams receive contracts, forms and reports in many layouts. Someone has to read each one and copy details into another system, which is slow and easy to get wrong.

Proposed product experience

  • Upload a document and see it beside the extracted fields
  • Each field shows where in the document it came from
  • Uncertain or missing fields are marked for review
  • A review queue where a person confirms or corrects the result

Conceptual solution

The tool would read a document, propose values for the fields a team cares about, and highlight the passage behind each value. Anything it cannot find or is unsure about would be placed in a review queue instead of being guessed.

Key features

  • Field extraction with a source highlight for each value
  • Clear marking of missing or low-confidence fields
  • A human review queue and an audit of changes
  • Export of confirmed data to a spreadsheet or other system
  • Handling of document access with privacy in mind

Potential benefits

  • Intended to reduce repetitive copying between documents and systems
  • Intended to make checking a value faster by showing its source
  • Hypothesis: a visible review step may build trust in the extracted data

These are intended outcomes and hypotheses. They are not verified results.

Technologies

Document text extraction and OCRLanguage-model serviceStructured data validationReview interfaceSecure storage and access control

Design and engineering approach

  • Show the source passage next to every extracted value
  • Treat uncertainty as a feature: flag it, never hide it
  • Keep a person in charge of final confirmation

Potential applications

  • Contract and agreement review
  • Invoice and form intake
  • Report summarisation for internal teams

Future implementation considerations

  • Test on real, anonymised documents of each type
  • Agree who may access sensitive documents and how they are stored
  • Decide which fields must always be confirmed by a person
