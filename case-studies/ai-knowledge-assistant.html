Company Knowledge Assistant

A concept for an internal assistant that answers team questions from approved company pages and always shows its sources.

Concept project — demonstration only

CategoryAI Applications
StatusConcept, not a client project
Related serviceAI Agents
This concept explores how a team could ask questions in plain language and receive answers grounded only in the documents the company has approved.

The hypothetical problem

Useful know-how is spread across handbooks, wikis and chat threads. New and busy team members interrupt colleagues to find answers that already exist somewhere.

Proposed product experience

  • A single question box for everyday questions
  • Short answers with links to the pages they came from
  • Suggested related questions
  • A clear message when the answer is not in the approved pages

Conceptual solution

The assistant would search an approved set of internal content, write an answer from what it finds and show the sources. If nothing relevant is found it would say so and suggest who to ask, instead of inventing an answer.

Key features

  • Answers limited to approved sources
  • Source links on every answer
  • Respect for who may see which documents
  • An admin view of unanswered questions to improve the content
  • A simple way to report a wrong answer

Potential benefits

  • Intended to reduce time spent hunting for known information
  • Intended to cut repeated interruptions for experienced team members
  • Hypothesis: showing sources may make answers easier to trust and check

These are intended outcomes and hypotheses. They are not verified results.

Technologies

Retrieval over approved contentLanguage-model serviceAccess-control integrationWeb interfaceUsage and quality review tools

Design and engineering approach

  • Limit answers to approved sources and show them
  • Design for the unanswered question as carefully as the answered one
  • Make correcting a wrong answer easy

Potential applications

  • Employee onboarding
  • Team handbooks and policy lookup
  • Internal product and process documentation

Future implementation considerations

  • Decide which sources are approved and keep them current
  • Respect existing document permissions
  • Review unanswered and reported questions regularly
