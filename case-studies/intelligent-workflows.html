Connected Workflow Intelligence

A workflow system that demonstrates how repetitive business tasks and information handoffs could be coordinated through automation.

Concept project — demonstration only

CategoryAI Automation
StatusConcept, not a client project
Related serviceAI Automation
This concept explores how an automated workflow could coordinate an incoming request from arrival to completion, with people stepping in only where judgement is needed.

The hypothetical problem

A request arrives by email or form. Someone reads it, copies details into another tool, asks a colleague to check it, updates a record and tells the team. Each handoff is a chance for delay or error, and nobody sees the whole picture.

Proposed product experience

  • One intake point for every request
  • Automatic classification and extraction of the key details
  • A confidence check that sends uncertain items to a reviewer
  • Records updated and the team notified once the item is cleared

Conceptual solution

The workflow would connect the intake, a classification step, a detail-extraction step and the systems of record. Items the system is confident about would move on automatically; unclear ones would wait for human review, with the extracted details and the original message shown side by side.

Key features

  • Single intake for email and forms
  • AI-assisted classification and detail extraction
  • Confidence threshold with human review queue
  • Automatic record updates and team notifications
  • Audit log of every step and decision

Potential benefits

  • Potential reduction in manual copying between tools
  • Potential for faster, more consistent handling of routine requests
  • Intended to give teams visibility of where each request stands

These are intended outcomes and hypotheses. They are not verified results.

Technologies

Workflow orchestrationAPIs and webhooksLanguage-model service for extractionDatabase and notifications

Design and engineering approach

  • Map the real process before automating any step
  • Make every handoff visible so people always know the status
  • Place human approval wherever judgement is needed

Potential applications

  • Request intake and routing
  • Approval flows between teams
  • Recurring reporting and reminders

Future implementation considerations

  • Confirm which tools need to connect and what their APIs allow
  • Define the fallback when a step fails
  • Pilot with one process before extending it
