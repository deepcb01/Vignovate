AI App Development

Custom AI-powered applications designed around real users.

Overview

What is AI App Development?

An AI app is software in which intelligence is part of the experience: it understands a request, finds the right information, drafts, classifies or recommends, and then hands control back to the person. VIGNOVATE designs and builds these applications for web and mobile around a clear business problem, not around a model.

The right architecture depends on your requirements, available data, budget and privacy considerations. Sometimes that means a language-model service with retrieval over your own documents. Sometimes a simpler search-and-rules approach is the better answer, and we will tell you so.

Problems it can address

  • Teams lose time searching documents, tickets and chat history for answers that already exist.
  • Customers wait for answers to routine questions.
  • Useful information is trapped in PDFs, emails and spreadsheets.
  • Manual steps such as sorting, summarising and drafting slow down capable people.

Suitable use cases

  • Knowledge assistants for internal teams or customers
  • Document intake and summarisation tools
  • Smart search across help, product or policy content
  • Drafting and review assistants with human approval
  • Classification and routing for incoming requests
Main capabilities

What we do

  • Intelligent interfaces

Honest limits

What to keep in mind

  • Language models can produce confident but wrong answers, so important outputs need review by a person.
  • Quality depends on the quality and permission status of the data used.
  • Some tasks are better solved with simple software than with AI, and we will say so.

FAQ

AI App Development questions

Do I need a lot of data to start?

Not necessarily. Many useful AI apps begin with documents and information you already have. We assess what is available, and what is missing, during discovery.

Will the app ever give wrong answers?

Language models can produce incorrect output. We reduce the risk with retrieval from approved sources, visible references, testing and review steps where accuracy matters, but no AI system is perfect.

Can the AI features live inside our existing product?

Often yes, through an API or an embedded interface. We review your current product to decide the best approach.
