Technical SEO

Technical foundations that help search engines find, read and understand your website.

Overview

What is Technical SEO?

Technical SEO is the groundwork beneath visibility: a site that search engines can crawl, understand and load quickly. VIGNOVATE audits and fixes these foundations, or builds them in from the start of a new website.

We treat SEO as engineering and clear communication, not as a trick. We cannot promise rankings or traffic, because results depend on competition, content quality, authority and time. What we can do is remove the technical obstacles and measure what changes.

Problems it can address

  • Pages are hard for search engines to crawl or are blocked by mistake.
  • Titles, descriptions and headings are missing, duplicated or unclear.
  • Structured data is absent, invalid or describes things that are not on the page.
  • A slow or unstable layout hurts people before they read a word.

Suitable use cases

  • A pre-launch SEO review of a new website
  • Fixing duplicate titles and canonical conflicts
  • Sitemap, robots and Search Console set-up
  • Structured data for an organisation, services and breadcrumbs
  • Planning topic clusters around what customers actually ask
Main capabilities

What we do

  • Technical audit

    Crawlability, indexing signals, redirects, canonical URLs and broken links, with a prioritised list of fixes.

  • Metadata and page structure

    Unique titles and descriptions, logical headings and descriptive internal links.

  • Structured data

    Valid JSON-LD that only describes information that really appears on the page.

  • Performance and accessibility basics

    Image delivery, layout stability and readable, keyboard-friendly pages.

  • Search Console set-up

    Verification, sitemap submission and a plain-language guide to the reports.

  • Content planning

    Topic ideas based on user intent. Keyword volumes are researched with proper tools before any claims are made.

Deliverables

  • A written audit with prioritised actions
  • Corrected metadata, sitemap and robots files
  • Validated structured data
  • Search Console set-up notes
  • A simple measurement plan

Honest limits

What to keep in mind

  • No one can guarantee rankings, traffic or indexing by a particular date.
  • Search engines change how they work, so recommendations are reviewed over time.
  • Technical fixes help most when the content itself answers what people are searching for.
Implementation process

FAQ

Technical SEO questions

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. We improve the technical and content foundations and report what changes.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It varies widely. New sites and competitive topics usually take months, and some changes show nothing at all.

Can you fix an existing website?

Often yes. We start with an audit so you know exactly what is wrong before agreeing the work.
