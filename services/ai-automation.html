AI Automation

Connect your tools and let software handle the repetitive steps.

Overview

What is AI Automation?

Automation removes the copying, forwarding, sorting and reminding that fills a working week. VIGNOVATE analyses how work moves through your business, identifies the repetitive steps and connects your tools so those steps run reliably in the background.

AI adds the ability to deal with messy input such as emails, forms and documents. We pair it with exception handling and human oversight, so unusual cases reach a person instead of failing silently.

Problems it can address

  • The same information is entered by hand into several tools.
  • Enquiries or documents sit unattended until someone notices.
  • Reports are assembled manually every week.
  • A process depends on one person remembering every step.

Suitable use cases

  • Capturing enquiries into one pipeline and notifying the right person
  • Reading and filing incoming documents
  • Syncing data between forms, spreadsheets and databases
  • Scheduled reports and reminders
  • Draft replies to routine messages, reviewed before sending

Honest limits

What to keep in mind

  • Automations follow rules and can fail on unusual inputs, so they need monitoring and a clear fallback.
  • Decisions with legal, financial or safety impact should keep a person in the loop.
  • Third-party tools can change their rules, limits or prices.

FAQ

AI Automation questions

Which tasks are worth automating?

Tasks that are frequent, repetitive and rule-based are good candidates. We rank them by the effort saved against the complexity and risk of automating them.

What happens when an automation fails?

We design error handling and alerts so failures are visible and recoverable, and a person is notified about cases the system cannot handle.

Do you replace the tools we already use?

Usually not. We connect your existing tools, and recommend changes only when a tool is the real obstacle.
