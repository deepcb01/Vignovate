AI Workflows

Connected workflows that coordinate information, tools and people.

Overview

What is AI Workflows?

Many business processes involve several steps, systems and people: an enquiry arrives, details are extracted, someone reviews, records are updated and a customer is notified. VIGNOVATE designs AI workflows that connect those steps into one coordinated, observable process.

Each workflow combines data sources, AI steps and review stages. People stay involved where judgement matters, and software handles the handoffs between them.

Problems it can address

  • Work stalls between teams because nobody sees the whole process.
  • Information must be re-keyed between tools.
  • There is no record of who approved what.
  • Busy periods overwhelm manual review.

Suitable use cases

  • Document intake, review and approval
  • Customer onboarding with checklists and verification
  • Request routing between departments
  • Content or asset review pipelines
  • Reporting workflows that collect, summarise and distribute updates

Honest limits

What to keep in mind

  • A workflow can only be as clear as the process behind it, so we map the real process first.
  • Edge cases and exceptions usually need a human route.
  • Integrations depend on the APIs and permissions your existing tools provide.

FAQ

AI Workflows questions

How is a workflow different from an automation?

A workflow connects several steps, systems and people, including approvals and status tracking. A single automation may only move data between two tools.

Can the workflow include human review?

Yes. We design review stages wherever judgement is needed and give reviewers the context to decide quickly.

Can existing tools stay in place?

Usually yes. We connect them rather than replacing them.
