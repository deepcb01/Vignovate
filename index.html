FAQ

Questions, answered.

Cannot find what you need? Contact us or message us on WhatsApp.

What does VIGNOVATE do?

VIGNOVATE is an AI innovation company. We design and develop AI-powered products, intelligent workflows, automation systems and web applications, and we offer supporting creative and growth services such as SEO, content, video and photography.

What types of AI products can you help build?

Examples include assistants and copilots, document and knowledge tools, workflow and approval systems, dashboards with AI-assisted insights, and intelligent features inside existing products. What is suitable depends on your goals, data and users.

Can you develop a custom AI-powered application?

Yes. We start by understanding the users and the problem, prototype the experience, and then build. We will also tell you honestly if AI is not the right tool for part of the problem.

Can you automate existing business workflows?

Yes. We map the current process, identify repetitive steps, connect the tools involved and design checkpoints for human review where mistakes would matter.

Do you build SaaS products?

Yes. We help with product discovery, interface design and development. Features such as user accounts or subscriptions are included when the product needs them, not by default.

Can you develop a website or web application?

Yes. We build responsive, accessible websites and web applications with technical SEO in mind, and we can prepare them for deployment on platforms such as GitHub Pages.

How does a project typically begin?

With a conversation about your goals and constraints. After that we agree what needs to be explored or built, and propose a scope in writing before development starts.

How is project pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, integrations, timeline and any ongoing support. After we understand your requirements we provide a written estimate. We do not publish fixed prices because projects differ widely.

Can you work with an existing product or codebase?

Often yes. We review the current product first, then agree whether to extend it, improve it or add AI features. In some cases we may recommend rebuilding a component, and we will explain why.

How can I discuss my project with VIGNOVATE?

Use the contact form, send a message on WhatsApp, or email us. Tell us what you want to build and we will reply with questions and suggested next steps.
